
TRANSFORMER
From their paper:

"We consider the encoder-decoder Transformer model from Vaswani et al. (2017)
with 6 layers and 8 attention heads per layer, as implemented by fairseq Ott et al. (2019). We scale
the size of the network by modifying the embedding dimension (dmodel), and scale the width of the
fully-connected layers proportionally (dff = 4dmodel). We train with 10% label smoothing and no
drop-out, for 80,000 gradient steps."


Experiment1 Settings:
-Ablation über unterschiedliche train sample Größen wählen (Spalte "n" aus den .csv)
-Adam optimizer mit default PyTorch values (noch herausfinden, ob damals pytorch default values anders waren)
-Learning rate des optimizers: "For training transformers, we replicate the optimization procedure specified in Vaswani et al. (2017)
section 5.3, where the learning rate schedule consists of a “warmup” phase with linearly increasing
learning rate followed by a phase with inverse square-root decay."
-"We preprocess the data using byte
pair encoding (BPE) as described in Sennrich et al. (2015)."
-Default layer initialization as provided by pytorch (at that time)
-No droput
-10% label smoothing
-80,000 gradient steps
-Trainingset: IWSLT‘14 de-en (varying amounts of samples. One sample conists of one de-en sentence pair.)
-Crossentropy loss

Dieses experiment jeweils für die Model-specifications
